BIO

You can find my Zhejiang University faculty page here. For technical reasons, it has not yet been consistently indexed by major search engines; this is currently being addressed.

I’m a quasi-legal researcher at Zhejiang University, Guanghua School of Law. By ‘quasi’ I mean that I’ve increasingly refrained from conducting black-letter conventional legal analysis type of work but rather consider me perhaps more of a legal theorist and empiricist than conventional legal analyst. I won’t call myself a socio-legal or empirical researcher because looking law thorugh the lens of social science methods is just one way of doing research that I like.

With an interdisciplinary heart and given that most critical and social analysis would have to consider interventions – and law and public policy, I’m now more interested in considering how law responds to the techno-socio phenomenon – with current focus on algorithmic addiction, model deletion, memorisation, real-world testing –

I’m a data protection lawyer by training, and have recently revitalised my interest in some foundational issues such as transborder data flows – especially from EU to China and at a transnational level.

Before joining Zhejiang University, I also worked at Aston University as a Lecturer in AI law (2024-2025), Interdisciplinary Research Fellow in Law, Ethics and Computer at University of Birmingham (working with Professor Karen Yeung and Professor Andrew Howes) and doctoral researcher/guest lecturer at the University of Edinburgh (2016-2019).

In my research capacity, I previously worked full time at Tencent Research (2014-2015), Alibaba Research (2022-2023), and TikTok’s Privacy and Data Protection Office (2023-2024).

Formal bio (for talks and programmes)

Wenlong joins Guanghua School of Law, Zhejiang University in November 2025 as a Research Professor. His research focuses on data and AI regulation in transnational contexts, with particular attention to cross-border data governance, AI standards and compliance architectures and the law of free speech in platform-mediated environments.

Before joining Zhejiang University, Wenlong moved between academic and industry in ways that his work folds together legal inquiry, public policy engagement and lived complexities of industry implementation. He held several academic posts in the United Kingdom, including the University of Edinburgh (Guest Lecturer and Editor of SCRIPTed, 2017-2022), the University of Birmingham (Interdisciplinary Research Fellow in Law, Ethics and Computer, 2020-2022) and Aston University (Lecturer/Assistant Professor in AI Law, tenure-track, 2024-2025). In parallel, Wenlong worked extensively across several leading global technology firms, including TikTok (Research Lead, Privacy & Data Protection Office, 2023-2024), Alibaba Research (Senior Researcher, Digital Economies & Public Policy, 2022-2023), and Tencent (Legal Researcher, 2014-2015).

Wenlong currently serves as an associate editor at International Data Privacy Law, member of the UNESCO’s AI Ethics without Borders, and Scientific Expert of the International Panel on the Information Environment.He also carries forward research affiliation with the Edinburgh Centre for Data, Culture & Society (CDCS) and co-edits the Future Law book series at Edinburgh University Press. Deeply committed to advocacy and discursive practice, Wenlong has remained involved in the Oxford Price International Media Law Moot Court Competition for over a decade, where he has taken on the roles of mooter, judge and coach.

Wenlong completed his PhD in IT law at the University of Edinburgh, having previously undertaken his interdisciplinary LL.M. by Research (Law and Journalism) and LL.B. at the University of Political Science and Law (China).
